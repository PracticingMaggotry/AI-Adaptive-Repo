01
Estimate concept mastery

BKT updates each concept's probability of being known from eligible correct and incorrect responses.

02
Set cognitive demand

The concept’s BKT mastery probability sets its Bloom cognitive-demand target from levels 1–6. Unseen concepts begin with a foundational diagnostic target.

03
Refine with question evidence

When at least five eligible responses across three distinct items are available and posterior uncertainty is low enough, Bayesian item evidence can refine the BKT baseline by at most one Bloom level.

Understand the six Bloom cognitive-demand levels
1 · RememberRecall facts and information
2 · UnderstandExplain ideas in your own words
3 · ApplyUse knowledge to solve a problem
4 · AnalyzeExamine parts and relationships
5 · EvaluateJudge using evidence or criteria
6 · CreateDevelop an original solution or product
Concept signals Up to five concepts with the lowest current mastery estimates
How to interpret this: quiz score and adaptive cognitive demand are separate. BKT estimates concept mastery from eligible exact binary responses; the resulting Bloom target guides planning for the next quiz. The displayed target is a concept-level estimate; the quiz planner can refine it for the selected question format when enough format-specific evidence is available. Partial-credit and essay results remain in performance analytics and are not converted into binary BKT evidence.
